## How to use this handbook

This handbook gives the online sales and digital desk everything needed to start Century posts and reels on Instagram and Facebook: the brand, the rules, and 41 ready-to-shoot briefs across 8 content buckets.

## Brand foundation

Century is a trust brand: **Since 1980. Pure home food. Nothing less.** Every post should leave the viewer with one belief: Century food is what you would make at home, if you could check every ingredient yourself.

**Who we are (for bios and About posts):** a family food business since 1980 that makes jaggery, soya, makhana and everyday staples in its own plants, the same plants that supply ingredients to some of India's best-known food brands. Now on your kitchen shelf.

**Brand promise (from our pack):** every ingredient is selected with care, tested with rigour, and trusted enough to carry our name into your home.

**The underline:** the yellow line under "ur" in the logo stands for Century's continuing legacy of bringing premium-quality food into millions of homes and kitchens. Use it as a recurring motif, such as an underline that animates under key words.

## Claims rulebook

Say only what the pack says, word for word; anything else needs written QA sign-off first. One wrong claim can draw an FSSAI notice and damage the B2B customers who buy our ingredients.

| Product | Approved claims (exact pack wording) | Watch-outs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jaggery Powder | No added colour; No chemicals; Natural sweetener; Quick dissolving; 100% veg | It is still sugar (92 g sugar per 100 g on our label). Never imply sugar-free or calorie-free. |
| Soya (all 4) | High source of protein; 99% fat-free; 100% vegetarian; Vegan; Super soft chunks; Rich protein texture; 35% more protein than paneer* | Always show the * footnote basis; never round or change the 35%. Contains soy. |
| Makhana | Naturally gluten-free and plant-based; Naturally fat-free; Best for roasting and snacking; Selected seeds; Hygienically packed | "Fat-free" is true only of plain makhana, not a ghee-roasted recipe. |
| Besan | 100% unpolished chana dal; High in protein; Low oil absorption; Fine besan | Verified; keep the lab report on file. |
| Poha | Naturally gluten-free; Rich in dietary fibre; Easy to cook; Made from selected grains; Hygienically packed | "Rich in dietary fibre" is lab-verified; keep the report on file. |
| Pulses | Unpolished; 250+ quality parameters tested*; Hygienically processed; Source of dietary fibre; Naturally protein rich; Carefully selected grains | Keep the asterisk; explain the parameters only with QA's list. |
| Seasonings | Product name and ingredients as printed | No health claims. |

**Never say, on any product**

- Diabetic-friendly, safe for diabetics, sugar-free, controls blood sugar.
- Cures, prevents, treats, boosts immunity, detox, weight loss, burns fat.
- Organic or certified organic. Century packs are not organic for now.
- Any certification (FSSC 22000, NPOP, NOP) unless QA confirms the plant making that SKU holds it.
- "Healthier than sugar", "better than" any brand, or any competitor name or logo.
- The names or logos of our B2B customers.
- "Chemical-free" for pulses, besan or poha; only the jaggery pack says "No chemicals".
- "High protein" for makhana.

**Claims not printed on a pack**

- Every claim printed on Century packs is verified. Anything else needs lab values and written QA sign-off first.
- Jaggery: no iron or mineral claim without QA; never "rich in iron" or "cures anaemia".
- Pulses: get the list behind "250+ quality parameters tested" from QA before a post explains it.
- Soya: keep the calculation basis for "35% more protein than paneer" on file.

**The B2B trust line**

- Use: "The same jaggery that India's leading food and health brands use as an ingredient, now in your kitchen."
- Use: "We don't just make a brand. We supply ingredients to brands you already trust."
- Don't: "Used by" followed by any customer's name, unless that customer has given written permission.

## Community management

Reply to every comment and DM within 4 working hours, and move any complaint to DM within 1 hour. Be honest, warm and short; never argue in public, and never delete honest negative comments (hide only abuse and spam).

| Question | Approved reply |
| --- | --- |
| "Is jaggery good for diabetics?" | "Jaggery is still a form of sugar, so we don't recommend it as a sugar substitute for diabetes. Please check with your doctor." |
| "Is this organic?" | "This pack isn't sold as certified organic. Every batch is quality-checked before packing, and the QR on the pack shows which of our own plants made it." |
| "Why is your jaggery darker than others?" | "Natural jaggery colour changes with the cane and the season. We don't add colour or chemical whiteners to make it look lighter." |

## Approval, checklist and KPIs

Plan a week ahead, check every claim, and judge posts by saves, shares and clicks, not likes.

Approval flow:

1. Draft next week's posts by Thursday.
2. Check every claim against the Claims rulebook; anything not on the approved list goes to QA for written sign-off.
3. The MD approves brand-level posts (Section 1) and every paid campaign; product posts go live once the claims check is done.
4. Schedule in Meta Business Suite.
5. Review results every Monday.

Pre-publish checklist:

- [ ] Hook lands in the first 1.5 sec; subtitles burned in
- [ ] Every claim matches the pack or the approved list
- [ ] No banned words: diabetic, cure, sugar-free, immunity, organic, any customer brand name
- [ ] Nutrition numbers taken from the pack panel
- [ ] "Serving suggestion" on every prepared-dish visual
- [ ] Logo end card and brand hashtags in place
- [ ] CTA matches the phase (no "buy" before the link is live)
- [ ] Music from Meta's licensed library
