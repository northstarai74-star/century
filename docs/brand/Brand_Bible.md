## 4. Brand identity

### 4.1 Name and logo
- The wordmark is "Century", always with the ®.
- The underline under "ur" stands for Century's continuing legacy of bringing premium-quality food into millions of homes and kitchens. Never remove it or separate it from the wordmark.
- Primary version: Century Yellow wordmark with the underline, on Century Green.
- Light or brown backgrounds: Century Green wordmark with a yellow underline (as on the jaggery sachet).
- Always use the master logo files (AI, SVG, PNG) from the design agency. Never redraw, retype or trace the logo.
- Keep clear space around the logo of at least the height of the letter "C".
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour outside the approved versions, outline, add shadows, or place it on a busy photo without a solid green panel behind it.

### 4.2 Colours

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Century Green | #136C37 | Primary: backgrounds, frames, end cards |
| Century Yellow | #FDB913 | Logo, highlights, the underline, key numbers |
| White | #FFFFFF | Text on green |
| Claim-band tints | From pack files | Cream, light green, coral, lime, tan bands used per product variant |

Pack printing can shift colours slightly. For all digital work use the hex values above, and pick variant accent colours from the original artwork files, not from photos.

### 4.3 Typography
- Headlines: the headline font used in the pack artwork. Get the font names and files from the design agency.
- Fallback and body text: Poppins (Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold). Poppins also covers Hindi (Devanagari).
- Keep on-screen text large and short: under 7 words per frame.

### 4.4 Approved brand lines
- Primary sign-off: **Pure home food. Nothing less.**
- **Since 1980**
- **From Our Family to Your Kitchen.**
- Brand promise: Every ingredient we source is selected with care, tested with rigour, and trusted enough to carry our name into your home.

### 4.5 Master brand story (from the pack; use as written)
At Century, we believe that home food never needed a label to be called healthy food. Long before wellness trends and health claims existed, there was food made at home with pure ingredients, honest choices, and the kind of care that cannot be manufactured. Which is why we hold ourselves to a simple promise: every ingredient we source is selected with care, tested with rigour, and trusted enough to carry our name into your home.

Because the quality of what goes into your kitchen shapes everything that comes out of it.

Century — Pure home food. Nothing less.

## 5. Positioning and strategy

### 5.1 Positioning statement
For health-conscious, aspirational urban families who want to know exactly what is in their food, Century is the everyday food brand that has made pure, honest staples and snacks in its own plants since 1980, so home food stays as pure as it should be.

- **Price position:** affordable premium. Above loose and mass brands, below ultra-premium. Never discount-led.
- **Proof, not promises:** every claim is shown (process, testing, QR on pack), not just stated.

### 5.2 Who we talk to

| Persona | Who | Cares about | Hero that wins them |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Family Guardian | Woman 28–45, Delhi NCR, upper-middle class, runs the kitchen | Pure, safe food for kids and parents; no adulteration | Jaggery, Besan, Pulses |
| Protein Seeker | 22–35, working professional, fitness-aware, mostly vegetarian | Protein per rupee, quick meals | Soya range |
| Mindful Snacker | 25–40, office-goer, reads labels | Snacks without junk; control over what goes in | Makhana, Seasonings |
| Traditional Cook | 45+, sets the family's taste standard | Authentic taste, familiar uses | Jaggery, Poha, Pulses |

### 5.3 Message house
- **Core message:** Pure home food. Nothing less.
- **Pillar 1, Purity you can check:** no added colour and no chemicals (jaggery); unpolished (pulses, besan); 250+ quality parameters tested (pulses).
- **Pillar 2, Everyday wellness:** plant protein (soya); lighter snacking (makhana); a less-refined swap for white sugar (jaggery).
- **Pillar 3, Homemade goodness:** recipes and uses that make home cooking easier.
- **Proof points:** since 1980; own manufacturing plants; ingredient supplier to leading Indian food brands; QR on every pack; toll-free consumer line.

### 5.4 Competitive frame (internal only; never name brands publicly)

| Type of competitor | What they own | How Century wins |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Big staples brands | Scale, trust, distribution | We are the maker, not just the marketer; proof shown product by product |
| Certified-organic brands | The organic badge | Provable purity at an everyday price; organic only when our packs carry the mark |
| Snacking and protein start-ups | Buzz, flavours, bars | Homemade control (plain makhana plus your own ingredients) and protein through everyday cooking (soya) |

Some brands are both competitors and B2B customers. Never criticise any brand in public.

### 5.5 What Century is not
- Not ultra-premium, and not a discount brand.
- Not organic: Century packs are not organic for now.
- Not a health-claims brand. We show purity; we don't promise cures.
- Not a trader. Every message reinforces that we make our own food.

### 5.6 Geography and channels
- **Geography:** Delhi NCR first; then Chandigarh tricity, Punjab and Haryana Tier 1 cities; then other North and West India Tier 1 and Tier 2 cities.
- **Offline:** general trade and modern trade, led by the sales team.
- **Online (Tarun):** thecentury.in goes live on 20 Oct 2026 as a brand-information site; online sales start later, on a date the MD will set. Meta drives people to the website and to WhatsApp, where Tarun handles enquiries and, once sales open, orders. Marketplaces and quick commerce come later.
- **Sachets:** jaggery and seasoning sachets sell through WhatsApp orders and to restaurants, hotels and cafes, not on shop shelves.

## 7. Claims rulebook

**Rule 1:** Say only what the pack says, word for word. Anything else needs written QA sign-off first. One wrong claim can draw an FSSAI notice and damage the B2B customers who buy our ingredients.

**Product watch-outs**
- Jaggery is still sugar (92 g sugar per 100 g on our label). Never imply sugar-free, calorie-free or "healthier than sugar". Approved framing: "a less-refined swap for white sugar".
- Soya: always show the * footnote basis for "35% more protein than paneer"; never round or change the number. Soya is an allergen.
- Makhana: "fat-free" applies to plain makhana only, not to a ghee-roasted recipe. Never call makhana "high protein".
- Prepared-dish visuals always carry "Serving suggestion".

**Never say, on any product**
- Diabetic-friendly, safe for diabetics, sugar-free, controls blood sugar.
- Cures, prevents, treats, boosts immunity, detox, weight loss, burns fat, guilt-free.
- Organic or certified organic. Century packs are not organic for now.
- Any certification (NPOP, NOP, FSSC 22000) unless QA confirms the plant making that SKU holds it.
- "Better than" any brand; any competitor's name or logo.
- The names or logos of our B2B customers.
- "Chemical-free" for pulses, besan or poha. Only the jaggery pack says "No chemicals".
- Superlatives we can't prove: "best in India", "purest", "100% healthy".

**Claims not printed on a pack**
Every claim printed on Century packs is verified. Anything not printed on a pack needs lab values and written QA sign-off first.
- Jaggery: no iron or mineral claim without QA; never "rich in iron" or "cures anaemia".
- Pulses: get the list behind "250+ quality parameters tested" from QA before a post explains it.
- Soya: keep the calculation basis for "35% more protein than paneer" on file for any challenge.

**The B2B trust line**
- Approved: "The same jaggery that India's leading food and health brands use as an ingredient, now in your kitchen."
- Approved: "We don't just make a brand. We supply ingredients to brands you already trust."
- Not allowed: "Used by" followed by any customer's name, unless that customer has given written permission.
- Internal note: our jaggery customers include Saffola (Marico), True Elements and Chaayos, among others. This is for context only and must never appear in public content, tags, hashtags or comments.

## 8. Voice and writing

| Trait | Sounds like | Never sounds like |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Honest | "Jaggery is still sugar. Use it like sugar, just a more honest one." | Miracle cures, fear-mongering |
| Warm, family | "From our family to your kitchen." | Corporate, cold |
| Knowledgeable | Practical kitchen tips, how we test | Lecturing, jargon |
| Quietly premium | Clean visuals, real product | Loud discounts, "cheapest" |

**Language:** captions about 70% English and 30% Hindi, natural for Delhi NCR. Hindi product names beside English ones.

**Do**
- Be specific and sensory: steam, crunch, tadka, the 4 pm craving.
- Show the proof: the plant, the lab, the QR, the unpolished grain.
- Teach something useful in every post: a hack, a recipe, a swap.

**Don't**
- Scare people about other food or other brands.
- Use superlatives we can't prove.
- Use emojis as a substitute for a clear message (one or two per caption is fine).

- Every post must look like it belongs with our packs: Century Green, Century Yellow, real food.
- Real product, real Indian kitchens, natural daylight, hands in frame, steam and tadka sounds.
- Show the actual product and pack. No stock images, and no food that doesn't match the pack.

## 11. Community management

Reply to every comment and DM within 4 working hours; move complaints to DM within 1 hour. Never argue in public. Never delete honest negative comments; hide only abuse and spam.

| Question | Approved reply |
| --- | --- |
| Is jaggery good for diabetics? | Jaggery is still a form of sugar, so we don't recommend it as a sugar substitute for diabetes. Please check with your doctor. |
| Is this organic? | This pack isn't sold as certified organic. Every batch is quality-checked before packing, and the QR on the pack shows which of our own plants made it. |
| Why is your jaggery darker? | Natural jaggery colour changes with the cane and the season. We don't add colour or chemical whiteners to make it look lighter. |
| Is soya safe? Hormones? | Soya is a widely eaten plant protein and is considered safe as part of a balanced diet for most people. If you have a specific medical condition, please check with your doctor. |
| Where can I buy? | Until sales open: Launching very soon in Delhi NCR. Follow us or join our WhatsApp list to hear first. Once sales open: Order on WhatsApp at [number]. |
| Why pay more than loose dal? | You're paying for what's not in it: unpolished, cleaned and checked on 250+ quality parameters before packing. |
| How long does it stay fresh? | Check the Use By date on the pack. Once opened, keep it in an airtight container. |
| Quality complaint | We're sorry, that's not the Century standard. Please DM us your batch number and a photo, and we'll sort it out quickly. |

**Complaint escalation:** move to DM within 1 hour; collect batch number, photo and place of purchase; share with QA within 24 hours; reply with a resolution within 48 hours; offer 1800 296 7999 or century@thecentury.in. Legal threats, media enquiries or health incidents go to the MD immediately.
